Marcus Vallance picked up a camera in the winter of 2015 on the Greek island of Kos, volunteering as refugees came ashore. He has photographed in camps and communities around the world ever since.

His photographs have been published worldwide and syndicated through Getty Images and SOPA Images. Boy Carrying a Bag of Grain was a Guardian photograph of the week, Child at a Window was shortlisted at the British Photography Awards, and in 2020 he was the sole exhibitor at the British Photography Awards at The Savoy in London. Based in Taunton, Somerset, he takes on portrait commissions across the UK and abroad, from press and album portraits to brand campaigns and private sittings.
