Portraits

Black and white portraits by Marcus Vallance: children in the camps at Idomeni and Kutupalong, musicians including Tim Wheeler of Ash and Gary Stringer of Reef on stage and off, athletes in Milan and faces from Rome, Zanzibar and Zambia. Black and white strips a picture back to expression and light, which is where a portrait lives. Many of these pictures were made in the first years of his work, following the refugee route north through Greece from Kos to the Macedonian border, and in the Rohingya camps of Bangladesh. Every photograph is numbered: to license one for editorial or commercial use, simply quote its title or plate number in your message when you get in touch.
