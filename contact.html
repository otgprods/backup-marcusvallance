Questions

Where is Marcus Vallance based?

Marcus Vallance is based in Taunton, Somerset, and takes on portrait commissions across the UK and abroad.

What kind of portraits does Marcus photograph?

Editorial, press and album portraits, brand campaigns and private sittings. His work is made on location rather than in a studio: in refugee camps, on stage, on the street and in the places people live and work.

Does Marcus travel for commissions?

Yes. He has photographed in Greece, Lebanon, Bangladesh, Zanzibar, Nepal, Ghana, Colombia, Italy and across the UK, and is happy to travel for the right project.

How much does a portrait commission cost?

Every commission is priced to the job. Tell Marcus who, where and when, and he will come back with an approach and a quote.

Does Marcus photograph musicians and artists?

Yes. Musicians he has photographed include Tim Wheeler of Ash, Gary Stringer of Reef and Matt Cardle, on stage and off, for press, artwork and campaigns.

Can I license one of these photographs?

Yes. All photographs on this site can be licensed for editorial or commercial use. Get in touch and include the photograph's title or plate number. His work is also syndicated through Getty Images and SOPA Images.
